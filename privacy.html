Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Jarvis is a private application used only by its owner, Abdelrahman Nasser, with his own Google account.

What data it accesses

With the owner's permission through Google sign-in, Jarvis uses the YouTube Data API to upload and update videos on the owner's channel, schedule them, and read the channel's videos, statistics and comments.

How the data is stored

The Google sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer. Channel data is used only to show the owner information about his channel. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Jarvis's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube data is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
